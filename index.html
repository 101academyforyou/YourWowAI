What you build with AI
deserves to be seen

A teacher builds a grading helper. A nurse builds a shift scheduler. A student builds a study tool. The people who need these tools are usually in the creator's own circle, but there's nowhere to share them, so they get posted in a group chat and forgotten. A stage for what you build with AI

Everything a post needs, and nothing it doesn't.

Show, don't tell

Every post needs a screenshot or a YouTube demo. Swipe or tap through up to 10 screenshots, and demos play right in the post. People get what your tool does at a glance, tap Try it to use it, and Learn it to see how you built it.

Cool or Not Cool

Instead of likes, people vote Cool or Not Cool. Honest feedback tells you who your tool works for and what to build next. The Top tab ranks the most Cool projects this week, this month or of all time.

Built around GitHub

Pick one of your own repos from a list, or paste any repo link, and the post fills itself in: name, description, website and the AI tools you used. Every post links back to the source code and shows the repo's GitHub stars; tap the to star it on GitHub.

Found by the right people

Pick the AI you built with (Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Cursor and more, or add your own) so people can find your work by tool or keyword instead of scrolling past it.

Instant feedback

Reply right under any comment and tap ⚡ on the ones you like, so conversations stay together. Get notified the moment someone votes Cool, comments, replies to you, shares your project or follows you, and when people you follow share something new.

Your next opportunity

Add your GitHub, socials or email to your profile. People who like your work can reach you, and your next collaboration might start here.

A friendly place to share

New accounts verify their email, which keeps fake accounts out. Report what doesn't belong, block anyone you don't want to see, and posts reported by several people are hidden automatically.

Browse by what you care about

Every project is tagged with what it's for: education, finance, health, games, developer tools and more. Pick a category and see only what matters to you.

Keep your post up to date

Shipped a new version? Edit your post any time: swap screenshots, update the demo, change the description or add categories. Pin your best 3 to the top of your profile.

Pass it on

Found something great? Share it to your profile's Shared tab with one tap, or send the link anywhere. Every post shows how many comments and shares it has.

Sign in your way

Continue with GitHub, Google or Sign in with Apple on iPhone, or use a username and password (verified with a code sent to your email, which also lets you reset a forgotten password). Link more than one to the same account.

Let your AI post for you

Create a private AI Webhook URL and hand it to Claude Code, ChatGPT, n8n or GitHub Actions. Your AI can share the project the moment it ships, and update the post when you release a new version.

Share in three steps

Takes less than a minute.

1

Add a screenshot or a YouTube demo

Pick screenshots, take a photo, or paste a YouTube link so people can see your tool in action.

2

Pick your GitHub repo and a category

Choose from your repos or paste a link, and we fill in the name, description, website and AI tools. Add a tutorial link if you wrote up how you built it. Tap the AI tools you used and choose up to 3 categories so the right people find it.

3

Share and collect Cools

Your post goes live right away. You'll get notified when people vote, comment, reply or follow you ⚡

Or let your AI share it for you

In Edit profile → AI Webhook, create your private URL and tap Copy AI prompt. Paste it into Claude Code, ChatGPT or any automation tool, for example:

Please share this project on YourWowAI for me. First GET YOUR_WEBHOOK_URL to read the instructions, then POST the project as JSON to the same URL: title, description, a YouTube demo link or screenshots, a link to try it, a tutorial link, the GitHub repo, the AI tools used and categories.

Prefer the command line or an automation like GitHub Actions? One request is enough:

curl https://yourwowai.onepointzeroone.org/api/hooks/YOUR_WEBHOOK_TOKEN \
  -F 'title=AI Receipt Tracker' \
  -F 'description=Snap a receipt and it sorts your spending.' \
  -F 'toolUrl=https://example.com' \
  -F 'githubUrl=you/receipt-tracker' \
  -F 'aiTools=Claude Code' \
  -F 'categories=finance,productivity' \
  -F 'media=@screenshot.png'

🔒 Anyone with your webhook URL can post as you. Keep it private, and regenerate it in Edit profile if it leaks. Up to 20 posts per hour.

FAQ

Does it cost anything?

No. Sharing is free and there are no ads.

What can I share?

Anything you built with help from AI: websites, apps, browser extensions, automations, small games. If you can show it, you can share it.

Why is a screenshot or video required?

So the feed is made of real, working things. Seeing the tool is the fastest way to understand it. Screenshots can be JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP; for videos, upload to YouTube (public or unlisted) and paste the link.

Do I need a GitHub account?

No. GitHub is optional. If your project is in a public repo, it saves you typing and adds a link to the source code. Sign in with GitHub (or connect it in Edit profile) and your public repos are listed for you to pick from.

What are Cool and Not Cool?

Our replacement for likes. Everyone gets one vote per post and can change it. Only totals are shown, so nobody sees who voted Not Cool.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time, inside the app. Your posts, comments and all your data are deleted immediately.

Can I edit a post after sharing it?

Yes. Tap "⋯" on your post and choose Edit post. You can change the text, links, YouTube demo, categories and screenshots.

Can I pin posts to my profile?

Yes. Tap "⋯" on your post and choose Pin to profile. Up to 3 posts stay at the top of your profile with a 📌, so visitors see your best work first.

How do I find projects in my field?

Use the category row on Home or Explore, for example Education, Finance or Games. Tap a category on any post to see similar projects.

How do I sign in?

Continue with GitHub or Google on the web and in the app, or Sign in with Apple on iPhone. You can also create a username and password. Already have an account? Link GitHub or Google in Edit profile → Sign-in methods.

Can my AI post for me?

Yes. In Edit profile → AI Webhook, create a private URL and tap "Copy AI prompt". Paste it into Claude Code, ChatGPT or any automation tool; it reads the instructions at the URL and shares or updates your posts. Anyone with the URL can post as you, so keep it private. You can regenerate or turn it off any time, and each account can post up to 20 times an hour.

Can I reply to a comment or edit and delete it?

Yes. Tap "Reply" under any comment to answer it in the same thread, and ⚡ to give it a Cool. Tap "⋯" next to your comment to edit or delete it. Edited comments are marked as edited. You can also delete comments on your own posts.

What does the paper plane do?

Choose "Share to my YourWowAI" to add the project to the Shared tab on your profile (the author gets notified), or "Share link" to send it anywhere else.
