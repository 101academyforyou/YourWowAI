Support & FAQ

Can't find an answer? Ask us on GitHub Issues.

1. Sharing

How do I share a tool I built with AI?

Tap the "+" in the middle of the tab bar, add screenshots or paste a YouTube demo link, fill in the tool name, description, try-it link and the AI tools you used, then tap Share.

Can I link a GitHub repo?

Yes. Paste owner/repo or a github.com URL into the "GitHub project" field and tap Import. The app fills in the name, description, website and any AI tools listed in the repo's topics (only fields that are still empty). Your post gets a GitHub button so people can see the source in one tap. Public repos only, and no GitHub login is needed. You can also add your GitHub account to your profile's contact links.

Can I edit a post after sharing it?

Yes. Tap "⋯" at the top right of your post → Edit post. You can change the tool name, description, links, YouTube demo, AI tools, categories and screenshots (remove, add or replace them). Every post still needs at least one screenshot or a YouTube link.

What are categories?

Categories say what a tool is for: Education, Productivity, Work & Business, Finance, Health & Fitness, Lifestyle, Marketing, Design & Creative, Developer Tools, Data & Analytics, Games & Fun, or Other. Pick up to 3 when you share. People can filter Home and Explore by category, and tap a category on any post to see similar projects.

Why is a screenshot or video required?

YourWowAI is about showing real work. A screenshot or demo lets people understand your tool at a glance, so every post needs at least one screenshot or a YouTube link.

What files are supported?

Screenshots can be JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP, up to 10 per post and 20 MB each. iPhone HEIC photos are converted automatically. For video, upload your demo to YouTube (public or unlisted both work; private videos can't be watched by others) and paste the link. youtube.com, youtu.be and Shorts links all work. The video plays right inside the post.

2. Interaction

What are Cool and Not Cool?

They replace likes. Everyone gets one vote per post and can change or remove it. Double-tap an image to vote Cool. Only totals are shown, so nobody sees who voted Not Cool.

Not getting push notifications?

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → YourWowAI and make sure notifications are allowed. After you sign out, that device stops getting notifications for the account; sign back in to turn them on again. Even with push off, the Notifications tab shows everything.

How can people contact me?

Go to Me → Edit Profile → Contact me and add your socials, GitHub, LINE, email and so on. Whatever you fill in appears on your profile and is visible to everyone, so only add what you're happy to make public.

3. Safety

How do I report something?

Tap "⋯" at the top right of the post → Report post, and choose a reason. The post disappears for you right away, and we review reports within 24 hours.

How do I block someone?

Tap Block in the "⋯" menu on their post or on their profile. You'll no longer see their posts, comments or notifications, and any follows between you are removed. You can unblock them from their profile any time.

4. Account

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?, enter the email you verified when you signed up, and we'll email you a 6-digit code. Enter the code and a new password, and you're signed in. For safety, you're signed out on every other device. Accounts created with GitHub, Google or Apple have no password, so just sign in the same way. If you signed up before email verification and can't reset it, contact us below.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Me → Edit Profile → Delete account at the bottom, enter your password and tap Permanently delete account. Your posts, media, comments, votes and all other data are deleted immediately and can't be recovered. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I change my avatar?

Go to Me → Edit Profile and tap your avatar or Upload avatar. Pick a photo or take one, and crop it to a square.

5. Contact

To report a problem, share an idea or report a serious violation, open an issue on GitHub Issues.
