YourWowAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

In short: we only collect what YourWowAI needs to work. No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data. You can permanently delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the YourWowAI app and website (together, "YourWowAI" or "the Service"). YourWowAI is a community for sharing tools people have built with AI. By using the Service, you agree to this policy.

3. What we don't collect

  • Your location, contacts, health or financial information
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA). We don't track you across apps or websites
  • No ads, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • We only upload photos you choose. We never read the rest of your photo library

4. How we use data

Only to run the Service:

  • Show your posts, profile and comments so others can browse, search, vote and follow
  • Notify you when someone votes Cool, comments, follows you, or when people you follow share something new
  • Handle reports and enforce blocks to keep the community safe
  • Keep you signed in and your account secure

We never sell, rent or trade your personal data.

5. What's public

Visible to everyone, including visitors who aren't signed in:

  • Username, display name, avatar and bio
  • Your posts (screenshots, video links and text), comments, and their Cool / Not Cool totals
  • Projects you share to the Shared tab on your profile, and each post's share count
  • Your follower and following counts
  • Contact links you choose to add (only the ones you fill in)

Never public: your password, which posts you voted Not Cool, who you blocked, what you reported, and your notifications and push token.

Don't add contact links you don't want public. You can remove them any time in Edit Profile.

6. Device permissions

The app only asks when you use a feature, and you can turn permissions off any time in iPhone Settings:

  • Photos: choose screenshots or an avatar to share
  • Camera: take a photo of your work or an avatar
  • Notifications: get Cool, comment, follow and new-post alerts

7. Third-party services

  • Hosting: your data is stored on the servers we use. The hosting provider may log connection IPs and times for security and operations.
  • Push notifications: delivered through the Expo push service and Apple Push Notification service (APNs), which receive your push token and the notification text (for example "Amy commented: …").
  • GitHub: when you tap "Import" for a GitHub repo, the app asks GitHub's public API about that repo (GitHub sees the repo name and your IP address). Only public information is read; we never need or get your GitHub account or password. GitHub's privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Sign in with GitHub, Google or Apple: if you choose one, you sign in on that service's own page and it tells us your account ID, name and profile picture (and Apple or Google may include your email, which we don't store). We only ask for basic profile access. Their privacy policies apply to that sign-in.
  • YouTube: demo videos are YouTube links. The app loads the video thumbnail from YouTube and, when you tap it, plays the video inside the app with YouTube's embedded player (youtube-nocookie.com), so YouTube (Google) sees your IP address and connection details, under Google's privacy policy. We don't store video files on our servers.
  • External links: "Try it" links and users' contact links open other websites or apps (such as Instagram or LINE), which have their own privacy policies.

8. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data until you delete it.
  • Delete a post: the post and its screenshots are deleted immediately.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Edit Profile → Delete account and enter your password. Your account, posts, media, avatar, comments, votes, follows, blocks, reports, notifications and push tokens are permanently deleted right away and can't be recovered.
  • Sign out: the push token for that device is removed, so it stops getting notifications for that account.
  • If our hosting provider keeps system backups, deleted data disappears when those backups are overwritten on their normal schedule.

9. Security

  • Passwords are salted and hashed with scrypt, so we can't know your password
  • On iPhone, your sign-in token is stored in the system Keychain
  • The production service uses HTTPS for every connection
  • Uploaded files are checked to make sure they really are images

No system is perfectly secure. If a security incident might affect you, we'll let you know as soon as possible.

10. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • View and edit your data on your profile and in Edit Profile
  • Delete posts or permanently delete your account
  • Turn off notifications and permissions in iPhone Settings
  • Ask us anything else, such as what data we hold about you, using the contact below

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Important changes will be announced in the app or on this page, and the "Last updated" date will change. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Open an issue on GitHub Issues or see the Support page.
